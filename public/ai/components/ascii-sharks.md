# ASCII Shark Ocean / 字符鲨鱼与鱼群

Character-built sharks swim, hunt and bend through schools of fish on a fine grid, with pointer avoidance, splash reactions and an optional translucent local composer.

字符鲨鱼在细网格上摆尾游动、追逐鱼群，支持指针避让与点击惊散，并可叠加半透明本地输入框。
