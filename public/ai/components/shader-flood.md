# Liquid Flood Cards / 液体注入卡片

Live marbled liquid fills each feature card from your entry point and drains toward your exit. Each card has its own three-color palette.

实时液体纹理从鼠标进入的位置铺满卡片，再向离开的位置收拢。每张卡片拥有独立的三色色板。
