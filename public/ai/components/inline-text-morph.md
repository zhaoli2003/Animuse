# Inline Phrase Morph / 内联词组变形

Click three inline pills to morph shared letters, resize the sentence, and swap illustrated stickers with a spring.

点击三处内联胶囊，相同字母连续重排，句子随宽度变化居中，贴纸图标弹性切换。
