# Album Cover Tunnel / 专辑封面隧道

A three-dimensional wall of real album covers: hover to lift, click to fly a cover into the center, then scroll or drag through the endless tunnel.

真实专辑封面组成三维隧道，悬停吸起，点击沿弧线飞入中央，滚动或拖动不断穿行。
