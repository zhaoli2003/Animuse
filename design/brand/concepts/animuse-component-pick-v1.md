## Concept

Two staggered cards represent a collection of reusable UI components. A cursor-shaped cutout in the front card expresses choosing and interacting. A vermilion rear card distinguishes the mark against the site's neutral interface. The monochrome treatment preserves the same meaning.

This sheet is a concept image, not a production favicon. The production vector widens the layer gap and simplifies the cursor tail for small sizes. Light/dark 16px, 32px and 48px renderings are recorded in `../animuse-icon-preview.png`.
